Nothing changed but the key. Same words, same maths, same tool. That is not a bug. A watermark is only visible to whoever holds the key, which is the entire design.

So no tool on the open web — ours included — can tell you whether a piece of text came out of Claude, Gemini or ChatGPT. Anyone selling you that is selling you a coin flip.

What Anthropic has actually committed to. Anthropic support article, read 29 August 2026.

Two different futures, and they are easy to confuse. If Anthropic publishes a key, the mathematics on this page starts working on Claude text and Opace can verify it here. If Anthropic instead opens a detection endpoint, Opace becomes a client of their service, the lab's mathematics is not used at all, and the answer comes from them rather than from us. 01 / Where the mark lives

Which words carry the watermark

A watermark is not hidden in one place. It is spread across the choice of nearly every word. Darker words are the ones pushing the reading up under the key you picked.

02 / Your own text

Try it with your own text

The maths runs in your browser. It needs a one-off 1.7 MB download, and nothing you paste is ever sent anywhere.

Before you do: this can only find the mark our own demo keys make. On text you did not generate here, the answer will be "no mark found", every time, whoever wrote it.
