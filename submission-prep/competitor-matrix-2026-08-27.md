## Confirmed positioning decision

The credible shared intent is **local-first AI content integrity**, supported by narrower phrases such as **content provenance**, **Astro content checks**, **WordPress content integrity** and **browser content checker**. Competitors validate demand around detector, humaniser, C2PA and provenance language, but Opace must not use `AI detector`, `humanizer` or `watermark remover` as a product-category promise for 0.1.0. The accepted proposition remains: evidence and named method states, not authorship or removal guarantees.

## Research boundaries

- Public fields can change after this date; recheck immediately before submission.
- GitHub stars/forks, npm downloads, WordPress installs and store users are different measurements and are not compared as one popularity score.
- Vendor accuracy and certification claims remain claims unless the programme's approved benchmark reproduces them.
- The target Opace repository, packages, directory listings and product routes remain unpublished; this matrix does not change that state.
