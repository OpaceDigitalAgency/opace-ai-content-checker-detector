What this tool cannot change

The neural model is a black box

The tier-3 score is a trained classifier (e5-small, int8). Its internals are learned weights over embeddings, not named rules, so there is nothing here to reweight or switch off. The only control this tool has over it is where to put the threshold. Its probabilities also sit in an extremely narrow band, so small threshold moves have very large effects — check the numbers rather than assuming a small move is a small change. Retraining it is out of scope for this tool.

Combination and escalation rules can override the weighted score

The score is not the whole classifier. These gates can raise a classification regardless of the weights you set below. Switch one off to see how much of the detection rate actually rests on it.
